# The Referral Connection: the plan

The report job names the next open step each night. A step that waits on Justin goes to his queue once, and the next step that needs nothing from him starts. Steps are milestones, not dates.

## L1. The library: all drafts, nothing needed from Justin

| Step | Job | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| The desk, worked on practice rows until the key is in | referral-desk | `daily/<date>/desk.md` says practice, with the listings, pairs and introductions it would make |
| A guide per pillar, eight in all | referral-guides | Eight drafts in the queue |
| The panel of 10 questions and the first read | referral-guides | `data/referral/panel.md`; the baseline written down |
| Prospects for a sample metro, each with a public source; first-touch and follow-up drafts | referral-growth | `data/prospects.csv`; drafts in the queue |
| The audience holders: chambers, associations, networking groups, local newsletters and podcasts, coworking spaces | referral-growth | `data/referral/dream100.csv`; first touches in the queue |
| Two weeks of posts, one a day, one pillar each | referral-growth | `content/drafts/` |
| The brief for the site's pages and form to cover all four kinds of deal | referral-report | One file in the queue (NEEDS-JUSTIN 9) |

## L2. Go live, one channel at a time

Each line is one yes from Justin, asked once, with the piece in front of him.

| Step | Job | The yes |
|---|---|---|
| The agent reads the site | referral-desk | The admin key (3) |
| The agent writes the site's records | referral-desk | "referral-site: bot", "crm-writes: on" (4) |
| The first guides published | referral-guides | `rc_guide` |
| Outreach to the launch area | referral-growth | The area and the sender (6); `rc_outreach_first`, `rc_outreach_followup` |
| Replies and public asks answered | referral-growth | `rc_reply` |
| The first post | referral-growth | `rc_group_post` |
| Introductions | referral-desk | `rc_introduction`, `rc_match_followup` |

## L3. Run it as a business

| Each | What happens |
|---|---|
| Day | Sign-ups by source, listings, connection requests, pairs by stage; every real sign-up listed and looked at for a match; tomorrow's focus from the weakest number |
| Week | Which source brought sign-ups that became listed and matched. Next week doubles the best and drops the worst. One decision, with its number, in `vault/Decisions.md` |
| Area | An area is full enough when a new sign-up there can be matched the same day. Then the report proposes the next area to Justin, once |
